sharp words or actionsa broken promise, “No, impossible”

One sharp word rarely starts a fight. A sharp word answered with another sharp word does.

1Don’t use sharp words.
2Don’t answer sharp words with sharp words.
3Calm the person and solve the real problem.A smile is not enough: fix the situation too.

Part 2 · Say it softly

“It’s company policy”: customers

“It’s company policy. There’s nothing I can do.”“I don’t make the rules.”“The system doesn’t allow it.”
Soft answer in 3 parts:1 · “I completely understand…”2 · “Our policy is…”3 · “But what I can do is…”
The same idea, with your team

“Because I said so”: your team

“Because I said so.”“That’s just how it is.”“Head office decided, not me.”
Soft answer in 3 parts:1 · “I know this isn’t easy.”2 · “Here’s why…”3 · “Let’s…”
Words that make it softer

Your softening toolbox

A soft startI’m afraid… · Unfortunately… · To be honest… · I see your point, but…
A request, not an orderCould you…? · Would you mind …ing? · Would it be possible to…? · I was wondering if you could…
Small softening wordsa bit · slightly · not quite · maybe · I think · it seems
Instead of “no”That might be difficult. · I’m not sure that will work. · Let me see what I can do.
“We”, not “you”Let’s… · We need to… · It looks like the tags weren’t… (not “You didn’t…”)
Thanks first, then the requestI really appreciate…, and next time could we…? · Thank you for…; one small thing:…

A real problem + sharp words = conflict. Don’t answer sharp words with sharp words.
