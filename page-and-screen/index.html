02Remember the patterns

Four ways to spotlight

What…

What + I + verb + is / was + focus

What I loved was the narrator's voice.

What surprised me was how short it felt.

It is / It was…

It is / was + focus + that / who + rest

It was the last chapter that made me cry.

It's the detective who steals the show.

The thing / reason…

The thing that / The reason I … + is / was

The thing that annoyed me was the ending.

The reason I read it was the cover.

All…

All + I + verb + is / was + focus

All I wanted was a happy ending.

All I need is a good book and tea.

What I loved it was…
It was the ending what…
All what I wanted…

Why do we need “that” or “who” in the It-pattern, but not in the What-pattern?
